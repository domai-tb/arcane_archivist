# Arcane Archivist Living Spec - 0.3

## Goals

- Deepen the established hub-dive-archive loop without introducing later-game systems.
- Add limited deck refinement so insight cards, archive bonuses, and dungeon rewards start forming simple synergies.
- Increase replayability through more meaningful choices inside the same early-game structure.
- Keep scope grounded: no base defence, no meta currency economy, no curses, no staff management, no co-op, and no full modding API.

## Player Experience

- The player accepts a request, prepares the archive, chooses a small deck adjustment, and enters the labyrinth.
- During the dive, the player encounters one or two opportunities to alter immediate tactics through card rewards, temporary upgrades, or route choices.
- After returning, the player decides which card option to keep for future dives and how it pairs with archive bonuses.
- The loop should start to show emergent strategy: a relic/tome setup encourages certain card choices, and those cards make different room risks attractive.

## Systems

- Deck refinement:
  - Add a small card collection separate from the active deck.
  - The player can swap a limited number of cards before starting a dive.
  - The active deck must always retain at least one movement or mobility card, one offence card, one defence card, and one utility card.
  - Deck size remains constrained to keep choices readable.
- Card rewards:
  - Successful dives or optional rooms can offer a choice of one card from a small reward set.
  - Reward cards should extend existing card verbs rather than introduce complex new subsystems.
  - Duplicates are allowed only if they support clear synergy and do not break balance.
- Card and archive synergy:
  - Add passive bonuses or card tags that interact, such as ward, flame, quick, heavy, or scholarly.
  - At least three synergies should connect archive bonuses to card behavior.
  - Synergies must remain understandable from item and card text.
- Dungeon depth:
  - Add one additional floor length variant or branch pattern.
  - Add one elite-style encounter or puzzle encounter that rewards using the right card/archive setup.
  - Each added dungeon theme or branch must change at least two of enemy pool, hazard set, or reward structure.
  - Keep run duration short enough for rapid iteration.
- Progression boundaries:
  - There may be persistent card unlocks from completed requests.
  - Do not add a general meta currency, upgrade tree, difficulty modifier system, or permanent library expansion economy.

## UX/Input

- Add a deck screen in the library with active deck, collection, card details, and swap controls.
- The dive start panel should summarize active request, archive bonuses, and active deck.
- Card reward choices must show current deck impact before confirmation.
- Card text should use consistent keywords or tags shared with archive items.
- If a card is modified by an archive bonus, the UI should indicate the modified value or keyword clearly.

## Content Scope

- 4-5 visitor requests total.
- 5-7 tomes total.
- 8-10 relics total.
- 10-14 total cards, including the original starter cards.
- 5-6 archive/card synergies, with at least 3 required for acceptance.
- 2-3 optional encounter templates beyond `0.2`.
- 1 elite-style enemy or puzzle encounter.
- No broad story chapters, staff automation, workshops, base attacks, curse ladders, or user-facing mod tools.

## Technical Notes

- Use a shared modifier pipeline for archive bonuses and card-specific effects so values shown in UI match values used in gameplay.
- Persist card collection, active deck, completed requests, archive placement, and unlocked reward cards.
- Keep card reward generation data-driven and constrained by request and dungeon context.
- Add validation for deck size and required starter capabilities so the player cannot create an unusable deck.
- Add lightweight automated checks for data integrity: missing card ids, invalid archive references, impossible request rewards, and invalid room template ids.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All `0.1` and `0.2` acceptance criteria still pass.
- The player can view a card collection and swap cards into a valid active deck before diving.
- At least five non-starter reward cards can be earned or selected.
- At least three archive/card synergies change card behavior in visible, understandable ways.
- At least one optional room or elite-style encounter is easier or more rewarding with a specific synergy setup.
- The player can complete multiple requests while carrying forward archive placement and deck choices.
- Save/load preserves card collection, active deck, archive state, and completed request history.
- Deck validation prevents the player from loading or saving an unusable deck.

# Arcane Archivist Living Spec - 0.2

## Goals

- Expand the `0.1` loop with one additional system layer: archive relic/tome combinations that grant passive bonuses.
- Make the library matter between dives without adding late-game base defence, broad meta currency, or narrative branching.
- Introduce player choice after a successful dive: where to place recovered arcane items and which passive benefit to enable.
- Preserve the playable loop from `0.1` and keep it stable.

## Player Experience

- The player still receives a request, dives for a tome, and returns to the library.
- Successful dives now bring back a tome plus one small relic.
- In the library, the player slots the tome and relic into archive positions.
- Simple adjacency or pairing rules grant a passive bonus for the next dive.
- The player can feel the effect of the archive choice immediately, such as starting with extra block, improved card draw cadence, or bonus damage against a simple enemy type.

## Systems

- Archive layout:
  - Add a compact archive grid or shelf layout with a small number of slots.
  - Each slot holds one item.
  - Tomes and relics can be placed, removed, and rearranged while in the library.
  - Placement is intentionally small scale: enough to prove adjacency without becoming a full layout game.
- Relic/tome combinations:
  - At least three passive bonuses based on item pairing or orthogonal adjacency.
  - Bonuses affect existing `0.1` systems only: cards, health, enemy damage, pickups, or dive rewards.
  - Bonuses are previewed before the next dive begins.
  - Active bonuses are recomputed from placement after every move and after load; they are not hand-edited save data.
- Requests and rewards:
  - Add a small pool of requests so the active request can vary.
  - Repeating request templates is allowed after the pool is exhausted.
  - Each successful request grants a tome and one relic selected from a limited pool.
- Dungeon dive:
  - Retain the short `0.1` dive structure.
  - Add one optional side room that can contain a relic cache, hazard, or tougher encounter.
  - Optional room rewards should make risk/reward visible without adding difficulty curses.
- Save state:
  - Persist completed requests, owned tomes, owned relics, archive slot placement, and the active bonus list recomputed from that placement.

## UX/Input

- Archive placement must support clear select, move, place, and remove interactions with mouse and keyboard.
- Hovering or focusing an archive item shows its name, type, and combination rule.
- The archive UI shows active and inactive bonuses distinctly.
- Before starting a dive, the entrance panel summarizes active request objective and active archive bonuses.
- During a dive, passive bonuses should be visible in a compact status area or pause/help panel.

## Content Scope

- 2-3 visitor requests.
- 3 tomes total.
- 4-6 relics total.
- 3 passive archive bonuses.
- 1 optional dungeon room category with 2-3 templates.
- 1 additional enemy, hazard, or interactable may be added only if needed to make a bonus meaningful.
- No staff, workshops, base defence events, permanent room destruction, curses, or multi-character systems.

## Technical Notes

- Represent archive items and passive bonuses as data, not hardcoded conditionals scattered across gameplay code.
- Archive bonus evaluation should run when placement changes and should expose a simple list of active modifiers to the dive setup.
- Keep modifiers narrow and testable: avoid open-ended effect scripting in this milestone.
- The dungeon generator should tag optional rooms separately from required progression rooms.
- Migration from `0.1` save data should default to an empty archive layout if no placement data exists.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All `0.1` acceptance criteria still pass.
- The player can place and rearrange tomes and relics in the archive.
- At least three distinct archive combinations activate passive bonuses.
- Active passive bonuses affect the next dive in observable gameplay terms.
- The player can complete at least two different requests across repeated sessions.
- Optional dungeon rooms sometimes appear and provide a clear risk/reward decision.
- Archive ownership, placement, and active request state persist after relaunch.
- Reloading a save recomputes bonuses from placement without duplicating or losing effects.

# Arcane Archivist Living Spec - 0.6

## Goals

- Add meaningful progression depth and replayability without bloating the core loop.
- Make long-term library growth support distinct strategies across multiple runs.
- Introduce optional difficulty modifiers that create replayable challenge goals.
- Give narrative and visitor progression enough structure to motivate repeated play.

## Player Experience

- The player sees a growing archive with strategic consequences, not just a larger inventory.
- Runs feel different because request mixes, relic catalogs, card unlocks, and curses reshape priorities.
- The player can pursue self-directed goals: specialize a wing, satisfy a patron faction, complete a relic set, or push higher-risk dives.
- Failure creates setbacks and pressure, but not dead saves.
- Replayability comes from build variety, procedural dives, optional modifiers, and unlock paths.

## Systems

- Rollout order:
  - Each of the following systems must be usable on its own during tuning: archive wings, relic sets, patron factions, curses, meta progression, replay records.
  - Start with one wing specialization path, one relic set, one faction chain, and one curse family before expanding counts.
- Archive wings:
  - The library expands into themed wings tied to knowledge tags.
  - Each wing has upgrade tracks that unlock station slots, relic capacity, card pools, and tag-specific bonuses.
  - Wings support specialization rather than universal upgrades.
- Relic sets:
  - Cataloged relics belong to small sets.
  - Completing or partially completing a set unlocks passive effects, new request chains, or card variants.
  - Set bonuses should encourage different layouts across playthroughs.
- Patron factions:
  - Visitor archetypes become factions with reputation tracks.
  - Factions offer request chains with branching rewards.
  - Favoring one faction can delay or alter opportunities from another, but should not permanently lock core content in this version.
  - Delays mean competing faction requests move back in the queue for at most two completed requests, then return if still valid.
- Curse modifiers:
  - Before a dive, the player can enable optional curses for better rewards.
  - Curses affect dungeon generation, card rules, archive pressure, or extraction risk.
  - Curses are selected from one family at a time in `0.6`; stacking different families is off limits until later.
  - Curse completion grants essence, rare relic chances, faction reputation, or cosmetic archive records.
- Meta progression:
  - Essence funds permanent library upgrades and wing unlocks.
  - Progression unlocks more options, not raw power alone.
  - Early upgrades should reduce friction; later upgrades should create new strategic branches.
- Replay records:
  - The archive records notable completions: relic sets, faction milestones, curse clears, and rare request chains.
  - Records are in-game archive entries and copyable text summaries, not external network sharing.
  - These records are lightweight but support memorable run stories.

## UX/Input

- Add an archive progression screen showing wings, relic sets, faction reputation, and unlocked card pools.
- Add a curse selection panel before dives with plain-language risk and reward summaries.
- Add run history or archive records accessible from the library.
- Add upgrade previews showing what a wing upgrade changes before spending essence.
- Keep all progression UI inspectable with keyboard, controller, and readable tooltips.

## Content Scope

- 3 archive wings based on existing knowledge tags.
- 2-3 upgrade tiers per wing.
- 4 relic sets with 3 relics each.
- 3 patron factions with short request chains.
- 6-8 curse modifiers.
- 22-28 insight cards total, including card variants or tag-linked upgrades.
- 8-12 new request templates that use faction, wing, or curse context.
- 6 archive record types.

## Technical Notes

- Keep progression definitions data-driven: wings, upgrades, curses, relic sets, and faction tracks should be authored as resources.
- Use additive unlock flags and modifier lists so save migration remains simple.
- Separate optional curse rewards from mandatory progression to avoid forcing difficulty spikes.
- Add validation for incompatible modifiers, such as curses that conflict with tutorial or story-critical dives.
- Track run summaries in compact save data to support records without storing full run logs.
- Design content IDs with future modding in mind; avoid renaming IDs after saves are created.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The player can unlock or upgrade at least one archive wing and see its effect on library or dive strategy.
- The player can complete a relic set and gain a visible passive or content unlock.
- The player can progress at least one patron faction request chain.
- The player can enable a curse, complete a dive with it, and receive the promised bonus reward.
- At least two viable strategic paths exist, such as wing specialization versus faction pursuit or curse farming versus relic set completion.
- Replay records show at least three types of notable accomplishment and persist across save/load.
- Progression order is testable: wings, sets, factions, curses, and records can each be validated without the others changing behavior.

# Hades II

## Overview
Hades II is the sequel to Supergiant Games’ acclaimed roguelike action title **Hades**.  Released into early access on PC in April 2024, the game continues the studio’s tradition of combining fast‑paced combat with narrative depth.  Players control **Melinoë**, the Princess of the Underworld, as she battles Chronos, the Titan of Time, with the help of various Olympian gods.

## Engine
- **Engine:** Supergiant uses its own proprietary game engine (built in C#/MonoGame) rather than Unity or Godot.  The same engine powered the original **Hades**.  It allows the studio to implement fluid 2D animation, dynamic combat and rich narrative systems.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Roguelike action combat:** Players fight through procedurally generated chambers, using melee and ranged weapons enhanced by “boons” from the gods.  The game emphasises quick, responsive combat and builds on the systems of the first Hades.
- **Boons and upgrades:** Characters receive temporary boons from Olympian gods that change their attacks and abilities.  Permanent upgrades are obtained via resources collected in runs.
- **Narrative progression:** The game features an extensive story with dialogue and character development that persists across runs.  New story elements unlock based on the player’s progress and choices.
- **New protagonist and setting:** Playing as Melinoë introduces new mechanics like “hexes” and a different perspective on the Underworld.  The hub area and side characters differ from the first game, providing fresh content for returning players.

## Reasons for Hype
- **Strong lineage:** The original Hades won multiple awards, including Game of the Year from several publications.  The announcement of a sequel generated immediate excitement.
- **Early access with frequent updates:** Supergiant released Hades II in early access, inviting community feedback and releasing updates with new weapons, areas and storylines.  This kept the player base engaged.
- **Narrative–gameplay blend:** The combination of roguelike replayability with an evolving narrative remains unique and compelling, encouraging both new and returning players to invest in multiple runs.

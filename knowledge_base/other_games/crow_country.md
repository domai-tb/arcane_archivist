# Crow Country

## Overview
**Crow Country** is a retro‑style survival horror game developed by SFB Games (the studio behind **Tangle Tower**).  Released in May 2024, it pays homage to PlayStation 1–era survival horror titles with low‑poly graphics, fixed camera angles and tank controls.  The story centres on a detective investigating the disappearance of the owner of an abandoned theme park.

## Engine
- **Engine:** Crow Country was developed using the **Unity** engine.  This choice allowed the developers to achieve the PS1‑like aesthetic while targeting multiple platforms.

## Sales
- According to a September 2024 article, Crow Country sold **over 100,000 units within its first six months** and performed strongly on both PC and consoles【590434461015435†L160-L168】.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Fixed camera survival horror:** Players navigate pre‑rendered environments using tank controls and solve environmental puzzles while conserving ammunition and health.
- **Retro aesthetic:** The low‑poly art style and grainy textures intentionally evoke early 3D horror games, appealing to nostalgia.
- **Exploration and story:** The game emphasises exploration of the deserted theme park, with notes and environmental clues revealing the backstory.  Puzzle solving and occasional combat create tension.

## Reasons for Hype
- **Nostalgic appeal:** Crow Country taps into nostalgia for PS1‑era horror, drawing fans of **Resident Evil** and **Silent Hill**.
- **Modern refinements:** While retro in style, the game features modern conveniences such as more forgiving save systems and quality‑of‑life improvements.  This balance made it approachable for modern players.
- **Indie pedigree:** SFB Games’ previous success with narrative‑driven titles built anticipation.  Positive reviews and word‑of‑mouth contributed to its strong launch.

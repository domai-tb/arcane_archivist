# Tiny Glade

## Overview
**Tiny Glade** is a relaxing, creative building toy developed by Norwegian indie studio Pounce Light.  Released in July 2024, the game allows players to construct idyllic medieval ruins, walls, towers and cottages on an infinite green meadow.  There are no objectives; the focus is on freeform creation and watching sheep graze in the background.

## Engine
- **Engine:** Tiny Glade was built using the **Godot** engine, demonstrating the engine’s capability for charming and polished visuals.  The developers chose Godot for its open‑source nature and flexibility.

## Sales
- Within a month of release, Tiny Glade sold **about 616,000 copies** according to statements from the developers【172404584741176†L140-L165】.  The game achieved high visibility on Steam’s charts and received “Overwhelmingly Positive” reviews.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Freeform building:** Players drag and place walls, towers and roofs with a mouse; the game automatically connects pieces and adds details such as ivy, fences and lanterns.  There are no budgets or limitations, enabling endless creativity.
- **Relaxing simulation:** Sheep wander the landscape, seasons change and a gentle soundtrack plays.  There are no enemies or fail states; the experience is akin to a digital zen garden.
- **Shareable creations:** Players can save and share their builds online, encouraging a community of creators.

## Reasons for Hype
- **ASMR–like relaxation:** Tiny Glade provides a calm escape from high‑stakes games.  The tactile building mechanics and soothing ambience made it popular among streamers seeking chill content.
- **High quality for small team:** The polish of the visuals and UI impressed players, demonstrating Godot’s ability to deliver beautiful games.  The game’s low price also encouraged impulse buys.
- **Viral builds:** Social media posts of intricate castles and cosy courtyards spread awareness and inspired other players to experiment.

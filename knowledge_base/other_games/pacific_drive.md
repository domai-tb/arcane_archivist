# Pacific Drive

## Overview
**Pacific Drive** is a run‑based survival driving game developed by Ironwood Studios and published by Kepler Interactive.  Released for PC and PlayStation 5 in February 2024, the game puts players behind the wheel of a station wagon traversing the treacherous **Olympic Exclusion Zone** in the Pacific Northwest.  The car functions as both lifeline and mobile base, and players must manage its maintenance while surviving anomalies, storms and supernatural events.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Run‑based driving:** Each run begins at the garage hub and sends players into the Exclusion Zone where they navigate procedurally generated road networks.  The objective is to collect resources and artifacts before returning to the garage.
- **Vehicle maintenance:** Players must repair and upgrade their station wagon by scavenging parts, managing fuel and balancing cargo.  The vehicle is customizable with tools, storage and defensive systems.
- **Anomalies and hazards:** The Zone is full of unpredictable anomalies—storms, gravity wells, rogue technology and hostile creatures—that can damage the car or change driving conditions.  Environmental storytelling and radio chatter enhance the sense of mystery.
- **Progression:** Resources collected during runs are used to upgrade the garage and unlock new technology, enabling deeper excursions into the Zone.

## Reasons for Hype
- **Unique premise:** Combining survival mechanics with vehicle management created a novel gameplay experience.  The idea of a car as your lifeline resonated with players and reviewers.
- **Atmospheric presentation:** The game’s audio design, radio chatter and environmental storytelling create a haunting atmosphere that drew comparisons to X‑Files and Stalker.
- **High replayability:** Procedurally generated roads and unpredictable anomalies ensure that no two runs are the same.  The early access roadmap and promise of future content encouraged players to return.
- **Platform deals:** Inclusion in PlayStation Plus and Game Pass significantly expanded the player base【153513481063926†L183-L196】.

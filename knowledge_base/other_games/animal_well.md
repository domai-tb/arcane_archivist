# Animal Well

## Overview
**Animal Well** is a nonlinear puzzle‑platformer and exploration game created primarily by solo developer Billy Basso.  Released in May 2024 for PC, PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch, the game features a mysterious labyrinth filled with hidden puzzles, creatures and secrets.  Its pixel‑art visuals belie a complex, interconnected world that encourages experimentation and discovery.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Nonlinear exploration:** Players explore a sprawling interconnected map, reminiscent of Metroid and Dark Souls.  Progress requires solving environmental puzzles and discovering hidden pathways.
- **Pixel art with dynamic lighting:** The game’s pixel‑art visuals are enhanced by advanced lighting effects implemented in the custom engine.  Lighting plays a role in puzzle solutions and atmosphere.
- **Limited combat:** There is no traditional combat; instead, players use items and environmental interactions to avoid or trick creatures in the well.
- **Hidden secrets and meta puzzles:** Many puzzles rely on cryptic clues and require players to think outside the box.  The game’s design encourages community collaboration to uncover deeper mysteries.

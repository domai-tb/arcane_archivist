# Enshrouded

## Overview
**Enshrouded** is a survival action RPG with voxel‑based building developed by the German studio Keen Games (known for **Portal Knights**).  Released in early access in January 2024, the game combines exploration, base building, combat and co‑operative multiplayer in a sprawling world covered by a deadly miasma called the Shroud.  Players must build shelters, fight enemies and uncover the mysteries of the world.

## Engine
- **Engine:** Enshrouded uses Keen Games’ proprietary **Voxel Engine** (also referred to as “Fledge”), which supports large open worlds, voxel terrain deformation and dynamic lighting.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Voxel world building:** Players can dig, build and sculpt voxel terrain to create bases and defensive structures.  Crafting and resource gathering are central to progression.
- **Co‑op survival:** Up to 16 players can join a server, explore the world and fight together.  The Shroud gradually encroaches, forcing players to build and defend safe zones.
- **Combat and RPG systems:** A classless progression system allows players to specialise in different combat roles.  Enemies range from human bandits to monstrous creatures and Shroud‑corrupted beasts.
- **Mystery and exploration:** The game features lore, quests and dungeons within the Shroud.  Clearing the Shroud permanently reveals new areas and story elements.

## Reasons for Hype
- **Voxel building meets survival RPG:** Combining base building with robust combat and exploration differentiated Enshrouded from other survival games.  Its voxel engine enables creative structures similar to Minecraft but with more realistic visuals.

# Content Warning

## Overview
**Content Warning** is a cooperative horror game from Swedish developer Landfall (known for Totally Accurate Battle Simulator).  Launched in April 2024, the game places players in the roles of aspiring content creators who venture into a procedurally generated haunted facility to film paranormal encounters.  After each mission, the footage is uploaded to an in‑game social media platform where views translate into in‑game currency and fame.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Found‑footage co‑op:** Players form teams and explore abandoned industrial complexes filled with traps, monsters and paranormal phenomena.  The goal is to film footage of spooky events without dying.  After returning to base, the recorded video is uploaded and players earn views and money based on its quality【975179126889748†L156-L189】.
- **Equipment and upgrades:** The team can purchase better cameras, lights and equipment to survive deeper expeditions.  Earning more views unlocks new items and cosmetics.
- **Permadeath and tension:** If players die during filming, their footage is lost.  The risk‑reward balance encourages players to push their luck for better content while fearing the loss of progress.
- **Procedural levels:** The facility layouts, monsters and events are procedurally generated, ensuring replayability【975179126889748†L156-L189】.

## Reasons for Hype
- **Novel concept:** Combining horror with social media satire captured the zeitgeist.  The found‑footage mechanic gave players a unique objective beyond merely surviving.
- **Streamability:** Content Warning was designed for streaming; players share their in‑game recordings and real‑time reactions, making the game ideal for platforms like Twitch and YouTube.
- **Low barrier to entry:** The affordable price and simple premise allowed large numbers of players to join quickly, leading to rapid adoption and viral growth.

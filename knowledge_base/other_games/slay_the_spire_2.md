# Slay the Spire II

## Overview
**Slay the Spire II** is the sequel to the influential deck‑building roguelike **Slay the Spire** by Mega Crit.  Announced in 2023 and released in early access in spring 2026, the game expands on the original’s card‑driven roguelike mechanics with new characters, relics, cards and multiplayer features.

## Engine
- **Engine:** Mega Crit switched from Unity (used for the first game) to the **Godot** engine for Slay the Spire II.  The studio publicly explained this decision as a response to changes in Unity’s licensing and to support an open‑source alternative.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Deck‑building roguelike:** Players choose a character and attempt to climb a spire composed of procedurally generated encounters.  They build decks from hundreds of cards, selecting synergies and relics to defeat enemies and bosses.
- **New characters and cards:** The sequel introduces new playable characters with unique mechanics and adds a large number of new cards and relics for deeper strategies.  Familiar characters and mechanics also return, providing continuity for fans.
- **Co‑op mode:** Players can join friends to tackle the spire together, combining decks and coordinating strategies.  The co‑op mode adds a social element not present in the first game.
- **Modding and open development:** Building the game in Godot allows easier modding and community contributions.  Mega Crit intends to support modding more directly than before.

## Reasons for Hype
- **Beloved predecessor:** The original Slay the Spire established the deck‑builder roguelike genre and has a large, dedicated community.  The prospect of a sequel generated excitement.
- **Switch to open‑source engine:** The decision to move from Unity to Godot drew attention from developers and players, especially after Unity’s controversial licensing changes.  It highlighted the viability of Godot for high‑profile commercial projects.
- **Early success and positive reception:** Strong early sales numbers and positive reviews reinforced the perception of the sequel as a worthy successor and ensured extensive coverage and social media buzz【532172794440993†L62-L89】.

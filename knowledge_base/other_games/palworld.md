# Palworld

## Overview
Palworld is an open‑world monster‑taming survival game released in early access in January 2024.  Players explore a vast map, capture creatures known as **Pals**, build bases, craft equipment and weapons, and survive environmental challenges.  The game combines elements of survival, resource management and monster‑collecting.  It quickly became a hit on PC and consoles.

## Engine
- **Engine:** Palworld uses **Unreal Engine 5**, which allows large open‑world environments, detailed creatures and scalable performance.  The game’s impressive graphics and physics are partly due to this engine choice【502757146391162†L155-L199】.

## Sales and Revenue
- The game experienced explosive growth after launching into early access.  It sold **one million copies in eight hours**, **two million in 24 hours**, **five million in three days**, **eight million on Steam in six days**, and **19 million players (12 million copies sold) within two weeks**【265900044537779†L65-L104】.  Within a month it reached **25 million players** and by February 19 2025 the game had **32 million players** across PC and Xbox【265900044537779†L65-L104】.
- Because Palworld is also available via Xbox Game Pass, the player numbers exceed units sold.  A report from GameDiscoverCo estimated Palworld had earned **over $442 million in gross revenue** by early March 2024【969741808179433†L52-L68】.  The game also peaked at **2.1 million concurrent players**, making it one of Steam’s biggest titles.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Creature capture and training:** Players can capture Pals that assist with combat, exploration and base tasks.  Each Pal has unique abilities and may be used to craft items, run factories or fight enemies【208694774030529†L88-L149】.
- **Open‑world survival:** The world features biomes, weather and day–night cycles.  Players must gather resources to build shelters, craft weapons and survive hostile environments.  They can use various weapons from simple spears to rocket launchers【208694774030529†L88-L149】.
- **Base building and automation:** Captured Pals can be assigned to tasks such as farming, mining or crafting.  Players design and expand bases with automated production lines.
- **Multiplayer and cooperative play:** Palworld supports online co‑op, allowing players to explore and build together.  The large open world and numerous Pals encourage emergent gameplay and cooperation.

## Reasons for Hype
- **“Pokémon with guns” narrative:** Palworld’s marketing emphasised a combination of cute, Pokémon‑like creatures and realistic firearms, which generated viral attention【208694774030529†L88-L149】.
- **Rapid scaling and streaming appeal:** The game’s early access success was amplified by streamers and social media.  The vast open world and humorous tone produced clips that spread quickly.
- **Controversy and attention:** Palworld drew criticism for character designs that resembled Pokémon, leading to legal threats from The Pokémon Company.  This controversy further increased public awareness【265900044537779†L65-L104】.
